Municipal Waste Minimisation and Circular Economy Planning

## OEG Graduate Programme Portfolio Case Study

**Role simulated:** Graduate Environmental & Infrastructure Analyst  
**Organisation:** Mavhungu Local Municipality (fictional)  
**Tools:** Excel, quantitative analysis, optimisation and technical reporting

## Important disclosure
This is an independent portfolio case study. Mavhungu Local Municipality and all operational data in this repository are fictional/synthetic. The project is not presented as professional experience or work completed for a real municipality.

## Problem
A fictional municipality wants to reduce landfill dependence, improve waste-service performance and allocate a constrained environmental-management budget more efficiently.

## Objectives
1. Analyse waste generation and material composition.
2. Measure recovery, landfill dependence and collection efficiency.
3. Identify zones with higher environmental/service risk.
4. Compare potential waste-management interventions.
5. Allocate a R15 million illustrative budget using a transparent benefit-per-cost prioritisation approach.
6. Produce evidence suitable for technical advisory reporting.

## Key methods
- Waste-flow calculations
- Recovery and landfill-diversion KPIs
- Cost-per-tonne and fuel-intensity analysis
- Environmental risk scoring
- Scenario/intervention comparison
- Budget-constrained prioritisation

## Regulatory and policy context
The project is informed by South Africa's National Environmental Management: Waste Act 59 of 2008 and the National Waste Management Strategy 2020. The NWMS 2020 places circular economy principles at the centre of its approach and identifies waste minimisation, effective and sustainable waste services, and compliance/enforcement/awareness as strategic pillars.

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and does not represent measured municipal performance. Environmental benefits, diversion quantities and intervention costs are illustrative assumptions for analytical training. The optimisation is a portfolio prioritisation exercise, not a procurement recommendation or environmental authorisation assessment.
